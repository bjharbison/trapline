# Sample inputs

Three short documents to try Trapline on. **All three are fiction.** Every person, company, organization and agency in them is invented. Real cities appear only as settings.

- [`investigative-report.txt`](investigative-report.txt): fictional news investigation into a harbor restoration fund, its contractors and shell suppliers.
- [`threat-intel-report.txt`](threat-intel-report.txt): fictional threat-intel bulletin on a phishing campaign against logistics firms.
- [`sanctions-filing.txt`](sanctions-filing.txt): fictional sanctions designation notice for an export-control evasion network.

Paste one into Trapline, or use **Upload**. The offline engine handles continuous prose best. Title and heading lines in the bulletin and the notice will add a few noise entities, which is a fair picture of what it does with real documents.
