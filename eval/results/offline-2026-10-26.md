At `--max-entities 40`, entity recall on these three documents rises from 75.8% to 98.4% and precision drops from 69.3% to 64.0% (same draft gold). Most misses at the default setting come from the cap, not from the NER. Precision doesn't depend on the cap in the same way: header lines, sentence-initial capitalized words and partial names are the main false positives.
